FAQ

Questions, answered.

What pack sizes are available?

50g, 100g, 200g, 500g and 1kg.

Can I order retail and wholesale?

Yes. Retail orders use the cart. Wholesale enquiries use the business enquiry form.

Are prices fixed?

Prices are configurable in script.js. Add your final retail prices before enabling paid checkout.

How does payment work?

UPI opens from checkout only when a valid total amount is available. Final payment verification should be handled by a payment gateway/backend.

Can I contact support?

Yes. Use WhatsApp, phone, email or the Zyka Assistant Pro.
