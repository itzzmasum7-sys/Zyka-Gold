FAQ

What pack sizes are available?

50g, 100g, 200g, 500g and 1kg.

Are product prices shown?

No. Prices are currently not displayed. Retail orders are handled directly after enquiry.

Select pack size, enter quantity and customer details, complete the confirmed UPI payment, then send payment confirmation on WhatsApp.

Retail payment uses the displayed UPI ID after the order amount is confirmed. Wholesale payment is requested only after the amount is confirmed.

What is the delivery process?

Delivery and shipping details are confirmed with the customer during order confirmation. Wholesale delivery is handled through enquiry.
